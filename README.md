# Fourier Optics for Inference 04 — Inside a Photonic Link

The hardware primer for the photonic link module of deck 01 (arXiv:2608.21536): light as a carrier that a photodiode sees only as power; the Mach–Zehnder modulator and its three bias points, and why quadrature suits unsigned bits; the textbook IQ modulator; holding a bias with dither and lock-in; direct against coherent detection with a local oscillator and a balanced pair; one input vector followed through the whole chain as constellations; the DFT's PAM5 and 3×3 alphabet and its packing into PAM4; calibration, where input phase errors distort and output errors rotate; the transimpedance amplifier, the noise budget and lane skew; and what a simulator needs from all of it. Every concept has a figure, and every mechanism an animation driven by a tested model.

**18 slides** (00–17), each with at least one figure, **8 of them stepped or interactive animations** (play, pause, step, scrub, speed, reset; paused under reduced motion): the modulator's arm phasors, the transfer curve and its bias points, a dither-and-lock-in bias loop, the local oscillator's projection axis, constellations through the whole chain for any of the 16 inputs, the DFT alphabet, calibration, and lane skew on a five-level eye. The animations are drawn by js/fop04_models.js, a port of the analysis script analysis/link_hw/link_hw.py checked against it by analysis/link_hw/test_link_hw.py. Facts about the module come from its paper and from patents the paper cites; inferences and illustrative parameters are labelled on the slide.

Topics: MZM bias points, Coherent detection, Constellations, PAM4, TIA, Calibration, Skew.
